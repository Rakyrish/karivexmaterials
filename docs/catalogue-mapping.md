# Catalogue mapping

Every item in the supplied catalogue brief, and what it became. The
repeated "Construction & insulation" list was consolidated into these six
categories. Each product has exactly one canonical URL, `/products/<slug>`,
however many categories or applications list it.

Status key: **P** published · **D** draft (kept out of the public site, sitemap and enquiry form until confirmed).

## Search terminology

`backend/catalog/search.py` treats these as equivalent: fibre/fiber;
fibreglass/fiberglass/fibre glass/glass wool; aluminium/aluminum;
rockwool/rock wool/mineral wool/stone wool; styrofoam/polystyrene/EPS/thermocol;
fireproof/refractory/heat resistant; firebrick/fire brick/refractory brick;
cyclone/turbine ventilator/whirlybird; armaflex/elastomeric/rubber foam;
PU/polyurethane; PE/polyethylene; sisalation/reflective foil.
